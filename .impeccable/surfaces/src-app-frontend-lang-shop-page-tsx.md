# Shop (list + product page)

Scope: /[lang]/shop and /[lang]/shop/[slug], mobile-first (390px) then desktop. Visitor mode: Operate (browse, compare, add to cart), with Persuade on the product page (trust in homemade products).
Audience: women in Lebanon from Instagram on mid-range Android phones, slow data. Task: find a product, trust it (price, what's in it, when it was made, how to use it), add it to the cart, finish on WhatsApp.
Constraints: extends the pinned C1 · Sunlit Arch world (no concept roll). All content from Payload; fashion items never shown. No ratings, no scarcity messages. Owner confirmed: two products per row on phones; product details all visible as a calm list.
Unresolved: real product photos; shop mockup was not produced (owner reviews screenshots instead).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A small shelf in her sunlit studio: arch-framed products on paper, her own homemade masks read like a labelled jar (made on, best before, what's inside). Refuses the marketplace default of star ratings, badges, sale ribbons and dense filter sidebars.

OWN-WORLD: Limestone page head, paper shelf section with grain, bright-paper arch cards (the home rail card at grid scale), ink chips for categories, clay only on the Add to cart pill; label rows with sand hairlines; patch-test note on clay-mist.

STORY: She sees every product with an honest price, taps one, sees real photos, reads exactly what a homemade mask contains and when it was made, and adds it knowing she'll confirm on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: List: page title, category chips in thumb reach, first row of two arch cards. Product: full-width arch photo strip with dots, name in display face, price, Add to cart above the fold.

FORM: Extension of C1 · Sunlit Arch (pinned). Seed key: none (pinned).

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the photo strip swipes in arch frames (scroll-snap, mirrored in RTL); Add to cart answers inline with "Added to your cart · View cart".

FINISH: screenshots EN/AR mobile + desktop for owner approval before the cart is built.
