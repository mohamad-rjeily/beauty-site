# Home page

Scope: public home page, /en and /ar, mobile-first (390px) then desktop. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: women in Lebanon arriving from Instagram on mid-range Android phones. Action: book (WhatsApp until phase 4) or browse products. Proof: her real work only; no data means an empty state, never invented proof.
Constraints: approved design "C1 · Sunlit Arch" is the pinned world; motion follows design/motion-notes.md; all content from Payload.
Unresolved: real photos, reviews, prices and brand name are not yet provided.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A sunlit limestone studio seen through arches: her photo and her hands lead, and every section is a doorway into one of her two crafts. Refuses the category default of a pink carousel hero, icon-card service grid and stock-photo testimonials.

OWN-WORLD: Limestone ground, paper cards, one terracotta field that owns the services section, sage for WhatsApp and Arabic accents, earth-brown footer. Arch-topped photo frames, pill actions, layered soft shadows, pre-rendered paper grain. Fraunces light with italic emphasis over Manrope; Amiri over Readex Pro in Arabic; a handwritten signature.

STORY: She understands this is one specialist for makeup and skin, sees real work and results, believes the prices and reviews are real, and books on WhatsApp in two taps from any screen.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed portrait photo to 620px; oversized 88px Fraunces headline "Beauty / rooted / in care." whose last line crosses the photo edge onto limestone; rotating terracotta Book badge at the photo's inline-end edge; tagline row with the Arabic line; sticky Book + WhatsApp bar at the bottom edge.

FORM: Approved design C1 · Sunlit Arch, pinned by the brief (no concept roll; brief-pinned direction beats the roll). Seed key: none (pinned).

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the before/after arch slider, one sweep on first view, then drag with a soft spring; mirrored in RTL.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
